# Persuasion Framework

**ROI:** 8.0/10 - Applies proven psychology principles in 15min

Uses Cialdini's 6 principles + behavioral economics for persuasive content.

---

## Cialdini's 6 Principles

### 1. Reciprocity
**Principle:** People feel obligated to return favors
**Application:** Give value upfront (free trial, ebook, tool)
**Example:** "Download our free sales playbook (no email required)"

### 2. Scarcity
**Principle:** People want what's limited
**Application:** Time-limited offers, low stock alerts
**Example:** "Only 3 spots left for this month" (must be true)

### 3. Authority
**Principle:** People trust experts
**Application:** Credentials, certifications, case studies
**Example:** "Used by 500+ Fortune 500 companies"

### 4. Consistency
**Principle:** People align actions with commitments
**Application:** Small yes → big yes (micro-commitments)
**Example:** "Start with free tier" → "Upgrade to paid"

### 5. Liking
**Principle:** People buy from those they like
**Application:** Relatability, shared values, humor
**Example:** "We're founders too — we get the struggle"

### 6. Social Proof
**Principle:** People follow the crowd
**Application:** Reviews, testimonials, user counts
**Example:** "Join 10,000+ happy customers"

---

## Behavioral Economics Add-Ons

**Loss Aversion:** Frame as avoiding loss vs gaining benefit
- Bad: "Save $100/month"
- Good: "Stop wasting $100/month on manual work"

**Anchoring:** Show high price first
- "$1000/mo Enterprise plan" → "$99/mo Starter plan (you save 90%!)"

**Default Bias:** Pre-select the option you want
- Checkout: Annual billing pre-selected (vs monthly)

---

## Copywriting Application

**Before (weak):**
"Our CRM helps sales teams close more deals."

**After (persuasion-optimized):**
"Join 5,000+ sales teams (social proof) who've cut their sales cycle by 30% (authority + specificity). Start free today (reciprocity + low commitment) — no credit card required. Only 10 spots left this week (scarcity)."

---

## Ethical Guidelines

✅ **Do:** Use truthful scarcity (real limited spots)
❌ **Don't:** Fake urgency (fake countdown timers)

✅ **Do:** Highlight real social proof (verified reviews)
❌ **Don't:** Fabricate testimonials
