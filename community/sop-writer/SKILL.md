## Example: Customer Onboarding SOP

**Purpose:** Ensure every new customer gets consistent, high-quality onboarding

**Steps:**
1. **Send welcome email** (Day 0, 5 min)
   - Use template: [link]
   - Include: Login credentials, kickoff meeting link
2. **Kickoff call** (Day 1, 60 min)
   - Agenda: Goals, timeline, success metrics
   - Assign CSM + technical POC
3. **Technical setup** (Day 1-3, 2 hours)
   - IT provisions accounts
   - CSM sends training schedule
4. **First QBR scheduled** (Day 30, 5 min)
   - Calendar invite sent
   - Pre-read deck prepared

**Quality Check:** Customer rates onboarding >8/10 in survey
