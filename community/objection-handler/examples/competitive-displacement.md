# Competitive Displacement - Incumbent vs Challenger

**Context:**
- Mid-market company ($50M revenue)
- Using [Market Leader] for 3 years
- Contract renewal in 4 months
- Pain: expensive, missing features, slow support
- MEDDIC: Champion (VP Ops), Economic Buyer (CFO skeptical)

**Objection:**
CFO: "We've been with [Market Leader] for 3 years. Switching costs are too high, and they just cut us a 30% renewal discount."

---

## Response Strategy

### 1. Acknowledge (Don't attack competitor)
"[Market Leader] is a solid platform — there's a reason you've used them for 3 years. And a 30% discount shows they value your business."

**Why:**
- Attacking competitor = defensive
- Acknowledge their track record = credible

---

### 2. Reframe (Switching cost vs opportunity cost)

"Two cost analyses matter here:

**Analysis 1: Switching costs** (one-time)
- Migration: 40 hours internal + 20 hours our team = **$15K** (we cover our time)
- Training: 2-day workshop for 10 users = **$5K**
- Risk buffer: 10% contingency = **$2K**
- **Total: $22K one-time**

**Analysis 2: Opportunity cost** (annual, recurring)

What's [Market Leader] costing you beyond the renewal price?

From your champion's input:
- Manual workarounds for [missing feature]: **80 hours/month** (2 FTEs)
  - Cost: $8K/month × 12 = **$96K/year**
- Support ticket resolution time: **5 days average**
  - Downtime cost (per your ops): **$12K/incident** × 8 incidents/year = **$96K/year**
- Lost deals from slow [workflow]: **3 deals/year**
  - Revenue impact: 3 × $50K = **$150K/year**

**Annual opportunity cost: $342K**

Even with their 30% discount (let's say $50K savings), you're still bleeding $292K/year in hidden costs.

**Math:**
- Switching cost: $22K (one-time)
- Opportunity savings: $292K/year
- **Payback: 27 days**
- **3-year value: $854K**"

---

### 3. Advance (Parallel run to prove it)

"Here's how we de-risk this:

**60-day parallel deployment:**
1. Run us alongside [Market Leader] (no disruption)
2. Pick your 3 highest-pain workflows
3. Measure time savings + error reduction
4. If we don't prove $100K+ annual value, walk away — no penalty

We've done this with 8 [Market Leader] customers. 7 switched, 1 stayed (unique compliance need we couldn't meet — totally fair).

Would a parallel pilot give you the data to make this decision with confidence?"

---

## Outcome
- CFO approved 60-day pilot
- Saved 60 hours/month in manual work (first 30 days)
- Support ticket resolution: 5 days → 4 hours
- Switched fully after 45 days
- [Market Leader] tried to match on features — too late

**Key lesson:** Incumbent advantage is real. Beat it with **opportunity cost math + risk-free proof**, not feature wars.
