# Authority Objection - Multi-Stakeholder Approval

**Context:**
- Champion: Director of Sales Ops
- Budget: $150K approved
- Gap: Champion lacks signing authority (needs VP Sales + CFO)
- Deal stalled 3 weeks

**Objection:**
Champion: "I love this, but I need to get my VP and CFO on board. They're slammed right now — can we reconnect next month?"

---

## Response (Champion Enablement)

### 1. Acknowledge
"Totally understand — exec bandwidth is the real bottleneck. Last thing we want is to rush this and have it die in a budget review meeting."

---

### 2. Reframe (Make selling internal easy)

"Let me help you build the business case so you walk into that meeting armed.

What format works best:
- **1-pager exec summary** (ROI math + 3 customer logos)
- **5-min slide deck** (problem/solution/proof/cost)
- **15-min live briefing** with me + you (I do the heavy lifting)?

I've done this with 40+ champions — the live briefing usually works best because:
1. Execs ask hard questions → I answer
2. You stay the hero (brought in the solution)
3. We get real-time feedback (vs email black hole)"

---

### 3. Advance (Control the process)

**Map the approval chain:**
"Walk me through the flow:
1. Who sees it first? (VP Sales)
2. What does VP care about? (Quota attainment? Pipeline velocity?)
3. When does CFO review? (Monthly budget meeting?)
4. Who has veto power? (Both? Just CFO?)
5. When's the earliest decision date?"

**Build the internal pitch:**

"For your VP, frame it as **quota insurance**:
- Current pipeline conversion: 18%
- With better lead scoring (our tool): 24% (benchmark)
- Impact: +$900K ARR with same lead volume
- Cost: $150K
- **ROI: 6x**

For your CFO, frame it as **sales efficiency**:
- Cost per closed deal today: $8,300
- With our tool: $6,200 (25% reduction)
- Payback period: 4.2 months
- 3-year NPV: $1.2M

Want me to draft both pitches for you?"

---

## Outcome
- Champion requested 15-min exec briefing
- VP asked 3 hard questions (pricing, integration, support SLA) — answered on call
- CFO approved on condition: 6-month pilot with exit clause
- Deal closed at $135K (10% discount for pilot terms)
- Pilot exceeded targets, expanded to $220K full contract

**Key lesson:** Authority objections = champion enablement opportunity. **Build their business case, control the process, offer to do the exec meeting.**
