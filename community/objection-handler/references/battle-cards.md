# Objection Handling Battle Cards

Quick-reference guide for live sales calls.

---

## The 6 Universal Objections

Every objection maps to one of these:

| Type | Root Cause | MEDDIC Gap | Fix |
|------|-----------|------------|-----|
| **Price** | Value not proven | Metrics weak | ROI calculator + case study |
| **Authority** | Wrong stakeholder | Economic Buyer missing | Map approval chain, get exec meeting |
| **Need** | Pain not urgent | Identify Pain weak | SPIN questions + cost of inaction |
| **Trust** | Unproven solution | Champion missing | Reference calls + pilot offer |
| **Timing** | Budget/priority mismatch | Decision Process unclear | Align to their fiscal calendar |
| **Competition** | Incumbent advantage | Decision Criteria unknown | Battle card + parallel pilot |

---

## 3-Second Objection Diagnosis

**Prospect says:** "We need to think about it"

**Decode:**
- Price concern? → Ask: "Is budget the main consideration?"
- Authority gap? → Ask: "Who else needs to weigh in?"
- Competitor? → Ask: "Are you evaluating other options?"
- Timing? → Ask: "What's driving the timeline?"

**Never accept vague objections.** Clarify → MEDDIC map → targeted response.

## SPIN Question Bank (Objection Prevention)

Use in discovery to surface objections early:

**Situation:**
- "Walk me through your current process for [workflow]"

**Problem:**
- "What's not working about [current solution]?"
- "How much time does [manual task] take weekly?"

**Implication:**
- "What happens if this doesn't get fixed in 6 months?"
- "How does [problem] impact [business metric]?"

**Need-Payoff:**
- "If we could reduce [problem] by 50%, what would that mean for your team?"
- "What would [solution] enable you to do that you can't today?"

## Objection Response Templates

### Template 1: The Metrics Anchor

"Let me show you the math:
- [Current pain metric]: [X]
- [Industry benchmark]: [Y]
- [Your solution impact]: [Z]
- [ROI calculation]: [Z - cost = value]

Does that math work for your situation?"

---

### Template 2: The Social Proof Stack

"[Similar customer A] had the same concern. Here's what happened:
- **Challenge:** [exact objection]
- **Approach:** [how they solved it]
- **Outcome:** [metric improvement]

Would it help to talk to them directly?"

---

### Template 3: The Micro-Commitment

"Sounds like we're not ready for a full commitment. What if we:
- [Pilot option 1]: 30-day trial, $X
- [Pilot option 2]: Parallel run, no disruption
- [Pilot option 3]: Reference call + exec briefing

Which de-risks this best?"

## Discount Decision Tree

**Prospect asks for discount:**

1. **Clarify:** "Is price the only thing standing between us?"
   - If no → Surface real objection
   - If yes → Continue

2. **Reframe:** "Our pricing reflects [value driver]. What if we structured this differently?"
   - Volume discount (commit to Year 2-3)
   - Feature trade-off (remove low-value modules)
   - Payment terms (quarterly vs annual)

3. **Conditional discount:** "I can do [X% discount] if you:
   - Sign by [date]
   - Provide a case study
   - Commit to [expansion trigger]"

**Never discount without getting something back.**

## Live Call Recovery Script

**When prospect drops a surprise objection mid-demo:**

"Great question — let me park the demo for a sec and address this properly.

[Repeat objection back to confirm]

Two things would help me give you a solid answer:
1. [Clarifying question 1]
2. [Clarifying question 2]

Then I'll show you exactly how [solution] handles this."

**Why this works:**
- Shows you're listening (not steamrolling)
- Buys time to think
- Clarifies the real concern

## Post-Objection Follow-Up (Email Template)

**Subject:** Re: [Objection topic] - data you requested

Hi [Name],

Thanks for raising the [objection] concern on our call. You asked for [specific proof point].

Here's what I found:

**1. ROI model** (attached)
- Assumptions based on your [metric]
- 3-year value: $[X]
- Payback: [Y months]

**2. Customer proof**
- [Company A]: [outcome]
- [Company B]: [outcome]
- Reference call available: [scheduling link]

**3. Next step**
If this addresses your concern, I propose [specific action] by [date].

If not, what other data would help?

[Signature]

**Key:** Specific, data-driven, time-boxed next step.

## Red Flags (Walk Away Signals)

Not all objections are worth solving:

❌ **Budget doesn't exist** - "We have no budget for this category"
❌ **No pain** - "Everything works fine, just exploring"
❌ **Wrong buyer** - "I'm just gathering info for someone else"
❌ **Endless delays** - "Call us in 6 months" (3rd time)

**Best response:** Politely disqualify, stay top-of-mind, revisit in 6 months.

## Practice Drills

**Weekly team exercise:**
1. Salesforce query: Lost deals, reason = objection
2. Pick top 3 objections
3. Role-play in pairs (AE vs SE)
4. Score responses (1-10)
5. Update this battle card with winners

**Individual prep (5 min before every call):**
1. Review prospect's industry/size
2. Predict top 2 objections
3. Pull relevant case study
4. Rehearse metrics anchor

**The best objection handling is objection prevention.** MEDDIC qualification = fewer surprises.
