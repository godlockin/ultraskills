# Strategy Consulting Framework

**ROI:** 9.0/10 - Applies MBB frameworks in 1h (saves $10K+ consulting fees)

Uses McKinsey/BCG/Bain problem-solving methods for business decisions.

---

## Problem Structuring (MECE Principle)

**MECE:** Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive

**Bad (overlapping):**
- Revenue issues: Product, Sales, Marketing, Go-to-Market

**Good (MECE):**
- Revenue issues: Acquisition, Retention, Expansion

---

## Issue Tree (Break Down Problem)

**Question:** "How do we grow revenue 2x?"

```
Grow Revenue 2x
├─ Acquire more customers
│  ├─ Increase marketing spend
│  ├─ Improve conversion rate
│  └─ Enter new markets
├─ Retain existing customers
│  ├─ Reduce churn
│  └─ Improve product quality
└─ Expand revenue per customer
   ├─ Upsell premium features
   └─ Cross-sell new products
```

---

## Hypothesis-Driven Approach

1. **State hypothesis:** "Poor lead quality is causing low sales conversion"
2. **Test:** Analyze lead source × conversion rate
3. **Validate/Reject:** Data shows paid ads convert at 5% vs organic at 20%
4. **Recommendation:** Shift budget from paid to organic channels

---

## 80/20 Analysis (Pareto)

**Question:** "What's driving customer churn?"

**Analysis:**
- 80% of churn comes from 3 issues:
  1. Poor onboarding (40%)
  2. Missing key feature (30%)
  3. Pricing objections (10%)

**Action:** Fix top 2 (70% of problem) first

## Recommendation Format

**Situation:** Revenue declined 20% YoY
**Complication:** CAC increased 50%, churn doubled
**Question:** How do we return to growth?
**Answer:** 
1. Pause paid acquisition (negative ROI)
2. Fix churn (implement health scoring)
3. Focus on expansion (upsell existing customers)

**Expected impact:** +30% revenue in 6 months
