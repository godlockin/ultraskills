# Champion Turnover Recovery - Financial Services

**Account:** Midsize bank, $200K ARR
**Trigger:** Primary champion (VP Operations) left for competitor
**Risk:** New stakeholder (SVP Strategy) unfamiliar with product, renewal in 4 months

---

## 72-Hour Response (Week 1)

### Intelligence Gathering

**Day 1:** Outgoing champion exit interview
- Called departing VP: "Thanks for partnership. Who should I work with now?"
- **Key intel:**
  - New owner: SVP Strategy (data-driven, skeptical of vendors)
  - Pain point: Board wants digital transformation metrics
  - Political context: New SVP cleaning house (reviewing all vendors)

**Day 2:** Stakeholder mapping
- LinkedIn research on SVP Strategy
  - Background: McKinsey alum, loves ROI models
  - Priorities: Cost reduction + operational efficiency
- Internal sponsor: CFO (our original exec buyer, still engaged)

**Day 3:** First contact (via CFO intro)
- Email from CFO: "[SVP], meet [CSM]. They've saved us $2M in ops costs."
- CSM follow-up: Value summary doc (not meeting ask yet)

---

## Re-Onboarding (Week 2-4)

### Week 2: Relationship Building

**30-min intro call agenda:**
1. "Tell me about your priorities for digital transformation"
2. "Here's what we've delivered so far" (metrics-heavy)
3. "What would make this partnership valuable to you?"

**SVP feedback:** "I need proof this works. Show me the data."

**CSM response:**
- Sent ROI model: $2M annual savings breakdown
- Scheduled usage audit: "Let's find underutilized value"

---

### Week 3: Quick Win Delivery

**Usage audit findings:**
- Only using 3 of 8 paid modules
- Fraud detection module (unused) = $500K annual value per industry benchmark

**Quick win playbook:**
1. **Training session:** 1-hour fraud detection workshop
2. **Pilot:** 30-day test on high-risk transaction segment
3. **Result:** Caught $80K in fraudulent transactions (first week)

**SVP reaction:** "Why didn't we use this before?"

---

### Week 4: Strategic Alignment QBR

**QBR with SVP + CFO:**

**Agenda:**
1. **Impact to date:** $2M saved + $80K fraud caught
2. **Roadmap alignment:** New features matching digital transformation goals
3. **Board-ready metrics:** Quarterly report template for Board meetings

**Deliverable:** Joint success plan
- Monthly check-ins (SVP's preference)
- Quarterly Board report (co-authored by CSM)
- Innovation partnership (early access to beta features)

---

## Results (6-Month Follow-up)

| Metric | Pre-Turnover | Post-Recovery | Change |
|--------|--------------|---------------|--------|
| **Health Score** | 82 | 91 | +9 pts |
| **Feature Adoption** | 38% | 75% | +37 pts |
| **Exec Engagement** | 1x/quarter | 1x/month | 4x frequency |
| **Renewal Rate** | At risk | 110% expansion | +$20K |

**Outcome:**
- Renewed at $220K (10% expansion)
- SVP became stronger champion than original VP
- Added 2 new use cases (fraud + compliance modules)

---

## Key Lessons

1. **72-hour response critical** - Champion turnover = 90-day vulnerability window
2. **Understand new stakeholder** - Different priorities, different value narrative
3. **Quick wins reset relationship** - Fraud detection win = instant credibility
4. **Executive alignment ≠ product training** - Board-ready metrics > feature demos

**Playbook refinement:**
- Added "stakeholder turnover alert" (monitor LinkedIn job changes)
- Created "exec persona" library (McKinsey types love ROI models)
