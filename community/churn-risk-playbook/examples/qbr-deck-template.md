# QBR Deck Template - Strategic Business Review

**Purpose:** Executive alignment, not product demo
**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Frequency:** Quarterly

---

## Pre-QBR Checklist (1 Week Before)

- [ ] Confirm exec attendance (reschedule if key stakeholders unavailable)
- [ ] Send pre-read deck 48 hours before meeting
- [ ] Review customer's recent news (funding, M&A, product launches)
- [ ] Update usage/impact metrics
- [ ] Prepare 1-pager leave-behind

---

## Slide Structure (12 slides max)

### Slide 1: Agenda + Business Context Recap

**Title:** Q[X] Business Review - [Customer Name]

**Content:**
- Meeting agenda (4 sections, 45 min)
- Customer's stated goals from last QBR (remind them what they said)

**Example:**
> **Your Q4 Goals (from Sept QBR):**
> 1. Reduce customer onboarding time by 30%
> 2. Increase sales team productivity by 20%
> 3. Improve forecast accuracy to >85%

---

### Slide 2-3: Impact Summary (The "Scoreboard")

**Title:** Business Impact Delivered - Q[X]

**Format:** Metric cards (visual, not table)

**Example:**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Onboarding Time                 │
│ 45 days → 28 days (-38%)       │
│ ✅ Beat 30% goal                │
└─────────────────────────────────┘

┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Sales Productivity              │
│ +24% (deals closed per rep)     │
│ ✅ Beat 20% goal                │
└─────────────────────────────────┘

┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│ Forecast Accuracy               │
│ 82% → 89% (+7 pts)             │
│ ✅ Beat 85% target              │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
```

**ROI Calculation (Slide 3):**
- Onboarding savings: $120K/year (15 FTE hours saved)
- Sales productivity: $480K incremental revenue
- **Total value: $600K vs $200K investment = 3x ROI**

---

### Slide 4: Usage Health Check

**Title:** Platform Adoption Snapshot

**Content:**
- Active users: [X] of [Y] licenses (XX% utilization)
- Feature adoption: [X]% of paid modules in use
- Trend: ↗️ Growing / ↔️ Stable / ↘️ Declining

**Visual:** Heat map (teams × features)

**Example:**
```
           Feature A  Feature B  Feature C
Sales Team    ✅        ✅         ❌
Ops Team      ✅        ❌         ❌
CS Team       ❌        ✅         ✅
```

**Call-out:** "Opportunity: Ops team not using Feature C (could save 10h/week)"

---

### Slide 5: Customer Voice (Wins + Challenges)

**Title:** What We're Hearing From Your Team

**Wins (2-3 quotes):**
> "This cut our reporting time from 2 days to 2 hours" - Sarah, Sales Ops

**Challenges (1-2):**
> "Wish we could integrate with [Tool X]" - Mike, Engineering

**CSM Response:**
- Integration roadmap update (next slide)
- Workaround available now (show how)

---

### Slide 6-7: Looking Ahead - Roadmap + Strategic Alignment

**Title:** What's Coming That Helps You

**Format:** 3 roadmap items tied to customer goals

**Example:**

| Feature | Your Goal | ETA | Early Access? |
|---------|-----------|-----|---------------|
| [Feature X] | Reduce onboarding to <21 days | Q2 | ✅ Beta invite |
| [Feature Y] | Improve forecast to >90% | Q3 | Join design partner program |
| [Integration Z] | Sales team request | Q4 | - |

**Ask:** "Which of these is highest priority for your team?"

---

### Slide 8: Expansion Opportunities (If Health Score >80)

**Title:** Unlock More Value

**Format:** Business case, not upsell

**Example:**
> **Current state:** Using our tool for sales team only (50 users)
> 
> **Opportunity:** Expand to customer success team (30 users)
> 
> **Why now?** CS team manually doing what sales automates (20h/week waste)
> 
> **Projected impact:** $150K annual savings, 6-month payback
> 
> **Pilot offer:** 60-day free trial for CS team, pay only if it works

---

### Slide 9: Success Plan - Next 90 Days

**Title:** Joint Commitments (Q[X+1])

**Format:** Action items with owners + dates

| Action | Owner | Due Date |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Roll out Feature X to Ops team | Customer (IT) + CSM | [Date] |
| Host power-user training session | CSM | [Date] |
| Complete integration with [Tool] | Customer (Eng) + Our Product | [Date] |
| Next QBR | Both | [Date] |

---

### Slide 10: Executive Ask (Optional)

**Title:** How We Can Better Support You

**Format:** 1-2 specific requests

**Examples:**
- "Can we get 15 min with your CFO to show ROI impact?" (expansion prep)
- "Would a case study help your Board presentation?" (advocacy)
- "Can we join your quarterly planning meeting?" (strategic alignment)

---

### Slide 11: Resources + Support

**Title:** How to Get Help

**Content:**
- CSM contact (Slack, email, calendar link)
- Support SLA (response times)
- Knowledge base / training portal
- Community forum (peer network)

---

### Slide 12: Thank You + Next Steps

**Title:** Recap + Calendar Invite

**Content:**
- Key takeaways (3 bullets)
- Next QBR date (already scheduled)
- Follow-up: "Action items doc shared by EOD"

---

## Leave-Behind: 1-Pager Impact Summary

**Format:** PDF, 1 page, executive-friendly

**Sections:**
1. **Impact at a glance** (3 key metrics)
2. **ROI calculation** (investment vs value)
3. **Next 90 days** (success plan)
4. **CSM contact** (how to reach us)

**Use case:** Customer forwards to CFO/Board

---

## Anti-Patterns

❌ **Product roadmap dump** - Features ≠ business value
❌ **No customer voice** - All vendor talking, no customer input
❌ **Skipping metrics** - Qualitative only = weak QBR
❌ **No action items** - QBR without next steps = wasted hour
❌ **Cancelled/rescheduled 2x** - Red flag, escalate to at-risk

✅ **Do instead:**
- Tie every slide to customer's business goals
- Start with customer context (5 min, let them talk)
- Quantify impact (metrics + ROI)
- End with commitments (mutual accountability)
- Pre-read deck (don't read slides in meeting)
