# Crisis Communications Playbook

**ROI:** 9.0/10 - Saves 8h → 1h with pre-built crisis response framework

Structured PR crisis response for data breaches, outages, scandals, or viral incidents.

---

## Crisis Response Timeline

### Hour 0-1: Initial Response (Golden Hour)

**Objectives:**
1. Assess severity (Tier 1-3)
2. Activate crisis team
3. Issue holding statement
4. Pause all marketing

**Tier 1 (Critical):** Data breach, safety incident, executive scandal
**Tier 2 (Serious):** Service outage >4h, lawsuit filed, viral negative press
**Tier 3 (Moderate):** Customer complaint trending, minor bug publicized

---

**Holding Statement Template:**

"We are aware of [incident]. Our team is investigating and will provide updates as we learn more. [Customer safety/data security] is our top priority. More information: [link]"

**Where to post:**
- Company blog (pin to top)
- Twitter/X (thread)
- Status page (if outage)
- Email to affected customers

---

### Hour 1-4: Investigation + Messaging

**Crisis team roles:**
- **Crisis Lead:** CEO or Comms Director (final approval)
- **Technical Lead:** CTO/VP Eng (root cause analysis)
- **Legal:** General Counsel (liability review)
- **Comms:** PR/Comms Manager (messaging + media)
- **Customer:** VP CS (customer outreach)

**Key outputs:**
1. **Root cause summary** (for internal use)
2. **Public statement** (approved by legal)
3. **FAQ document** (for support team)
4. **Media talking points** (for spokespeople)

---

**Public Statement Framework:**

1. **Acknowledge** (what happened)
   "On [date], we experienced [incident] affecting [X] customers."

2. **Apologize** (if appropriate)
   "We sincerely apologize for the disruption and concern this has caused."

3. **Explain** (root cause, without jargon)
   "A [technical issue/human error] caused [impact]. Here's what happened..."

4. **Action** (what you're doing)
   "We have [immediate fix] and are implementing [long-term prevention]."

5. **Contact** (where to get help)
   "Affected customers: [support email/phone]. Updates: [status page]."

---

### Hour 4-24: Media Management

**Inbound media requests:**
- **Spokesperson:** CEO (Tier 1), Comms Director (Tier 2-3)
- **Approved outlets:** Tier-1 media only (WSJ, Bloomberg, NYT)
- **Interview format:** Written statement > phone > video (most control)

**Talking Points (3-5 bullets):**
1. "Customer safety is our top priority"
2. "We've identified the cause and implemented a fix"
3. "We're conducting a full review to prevent recurrence"
4. "We're directly contacting affected customers"
5. "We take full responsibility and are committed to making this right"

**Never say:**
- "No comment" (sounds guilty)
- "It's not our fault" (defensive)
- "This never happens" (provably false)
- Speculate on root cause before confirmed

---

### Day 1-7: Ongoing Communication

**Daily updates:**
- Post update every 24h (even if "no new information")
- Blog post + Twitter thread
- Email to affected customers

**Metrics to track:**
- Sentiment analysis (social listening)
- Media mentions (positive/neutral/negative)
- Customer churn spike
- Support ticket volume

**De-escalation signals:**
- Negative sentiment declining
- Media requests slowing
- Support tickets normalizing

## Crisis Tier Playbooks

### Tier 1: Data Breach

**Immediate actions:**
1. **Contain breach** (isolate affected systems)
2. **Legal notification** (GDPR = 72h, state laws vary)
3. **Customer notification** (email all affected)
4. **Offer remediation** (credit monitoring, password reset)

**Public statement additions:**
- "We have engaged a third-party cybersecurity firm to investigate"
- "We are working with law enforcement"
- "No financial information was compromised" (if true)

---

### Tier 2: Service Outage (>4h)

**Immediate actions:**
1. **Status page update** (within 15 min)
2. **Engineering all-hands** (fix + root cause)
3. **Customer email** (acknowledge + ETA)
4. **SLA credit** (if contractual obligation)

**Post-mortem (public):**
- Root cause analysis
- Timeline of events
- Preventative measures
- Compensation plan (if applicable)

---

### Tier 3: Viral Negative Incident

**Examples:** Customer complaint goes viral, employee misconduct, offensive marketing

**Immediate actions:**
1. **Pause related campaigns** (ads, emails, social posts)
2. **Internal investigation** (gather facts)
3. **Public acknowledgment** (within 4 hours)
4. **Corrective action** (fire employee, pull ad, change policy)

**Apology framework:**
"We were wrong. [Specific mistake]. We've [corrective action] and are committed to [prevention]."

---

## Media Training Essentials

**The 3-Point Message:**
Every interview, repeat these 3 points (no matter the question):
1. [Customer commitment]
2. [Action taken]
3. [Prevention plan]

**Bridging phrases:**
- "What's most important is..."
- "Let me be clear about..."
- "Here's what we're focused on..."

**Dodging landmines:**
- Hypotheticals: "I can't speculate, but what I can tell you is..."
- Blame: "Our focus is on fixing this, not pointing fingers"
- Legal questions: "Our legal team is reviewing that. What I can share is..."

---

## Post-Crisis Review (Week 2)

**Internal debrief:**
- What went well?
- What went poorly?
- Playbook updates needed?
- Team roles clear?

**Public post-mortem** (if Tier 1):
- Blog post with full timeline
- Root cause analysis (technical but accessible)
- Preventative measures implemented
- Thank customers for patience

**Reputation repair:**
- Customer appreciation campaign
- Case studies of how you fixed it
- Industry thought leadership (share learnings)
