# Course Outline Generator

**ROI:** 7.5/10 - Saves 4h → 30min for instructional design

Creates learning-objective-driven course outlines with assessments.

## Course Structure

**Course Title:** [Name]
**Target Audience:** [Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced]
**Duration:** [X hours]
**Format:** [Self-paced/Live/Hybrid]

### Learning Objectives

By end of course, learners will:
1. [Action verb] [skill] (e.g., "Analyze sales funnels")
2. [Action verb] [skill]
3. [Action verb] [skill]

### Module Breakdown

**Module 1: [Topic]** (X hours)
- Lesson 1.1: [Subtopic] (X min)
  - Concept: [Key idea]
  - Activity: [Practice exercise]
  - Assessment: [Quiz/exercise]
- Lesson 1.2: [Subtopic]

**Module 2: [Topic]**
...

### Assessments

- **Formative:** Quizzes after each module (checks understanding)
- **Summative:** Final project (applies all skills)

## Example: "Sales Fundamentals" Course

**Audience:** New sales reps (0-6 months experience)
**Duration:** 8 hours (self-paced)

**Learning Objectives:**
1. Qualify leads using BANT framework
2. Conduct discovery calls with SPIN questions
3. Handle 5 common objections

**Module 1: Lead Qualification** (2 hours)
- Lesson 1.1: BANT framework (30 min)
  - Video: What is BANT?
  - Activity: Score 5 sample leads
  - Quiz: 10 questions
- Lesson 1.2: MEDDIC for complex sales (30 min)

**Final Project:** Role-play a discovery call (graded by manager)

## Bloom's Taxonomy (Learning Verbs)

- **Remember:** List, define, identify
- **Understand:** Explain, summarize, classify
- **Apply:** Execute, implement, use
- **Analyze:** Compare, diagnose, examine
- **Evaluate:** Judge, critique, assess
- **Create:** Design, develop, build
