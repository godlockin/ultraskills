# Data Storytelling Framework

**ROI:** 8.0/10 - Saves 3h → 30min presenting insights

Transforms data analysis into compelling executive narratives.

---

## Story Structure

**Setup:** Context + baseline
**Conflict:** Problem revealed by data
**Resolution:** Recommendation + expected impact

**Example:**
- Setup: "Our CAC was $1K last year"
- Conflict: "It's now $2K — we're paying 2x for same customers"
- Resolution: "Shift budget from paid ads (CAC $3K) to content (CAC $500). Save $150K/quarter."

---

## Visualization Principles

**1. One chart, one message**
- Bad: 10 metrics on one chart
- Good: Focus on the insight (e.g., "CAC doubled")

**2. Annotate key points**
- Add text: "CAC spiked here → paid ad experiment failed"

**3. Remove clutter**
- No 3D charts, excessive gridlines, or decorations

---

## Executive Summary (3 bullets)

1. **Finding:** What changed (CAC doubled)
2. **Impact:** Business consequence (-$150K profit/quarter)
3. **Action:** What to do (shift to content marketing)
