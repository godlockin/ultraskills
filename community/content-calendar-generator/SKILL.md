## Content Mix (Rule of Thirds)

**1/3 Thought leadership** (build authority)
- Industry trends, hot takes, frameworks

**1/3 Product education** (drive conversions)
- How-tos, demos, use cases

**1/3 Customer stories** (social proof)
- Case studies, testimonials, reviews

---

## Planning Process

**Step 1: Set goals**
- SEO traffic: +20% MoM
- Leads: 50/month from content
- Engagement: 1K social shares

**Step 2: Keyword research**
- Tool: Ahrefs, SEMrush
- Target: 10-20 keywords (difficulty <30)

**Step 3: Content themes**
- Pillar 1: Sales best practices
- Pillar 2: Product tutorials
- Pillar 3: Customer success

**Step 4: Schedule**
- Blog: 2x/week (Tuesday, Thursday)
- Video: 1x/week (Monday)
- Social: Daily
