# Unsticking Stalled Enterprise Deal - $500K ARR

**Deal:** Enterprise healthcare company
**Stage:** Proposal (stuck 62 days, benchmark = 21 days)
**Value:** $500K ARR
**Risk:** Red (3x velocity)

---

## Root Cause Diagnosis

**Manager review findings:**
- MEDDIC score: 6/10 (weak on Decision Process + Economic Buyer)
- Champion (IT Director) can't get CFO meeting
- Legal reviewing contract but no feedback for 45 days
- Competitive threat: Incumbent vendor offered 30% discount

**Customer pulse check call:**

AE: "Where are you in the approval process?"
Champion: "Stuck in legal. CFO wants sign-off but legal keeps asking questions."
AE: "What questions specifically?"
Champion: "Data residency, SOC2, liability caps. Honestly, I don't know how to answer."

**Real blocker:** Legal friction + champion can't navigate internal process

---

## Intervention (Week 1-2)

### Action 1: Legal Playbook (Day 1)
- Sent "Legal FAQ" doc (pre-answered data/security questions)
- Offered 30-min call with our legal team + their legal
- Result: Call scheduled, 15 questions resolved in 20 min

### Action 2: Executive Alignment (Day 3)
- AE requested CFO meeting (via champion)
- Champion nervous: "CFO doesn't do vendor calls"
- **Escalation:** Our VP Sales emailed CFO directly
  - Subject: "15-min ROI discussion - [Champion] recommended"
  - CFO accepted (exec-to-exec works)

### Action 3: Competitive Defense (Day 5)
- Learned incumbent offered 30% discount
- **Response:** ROI calculator showing 3-year TCO
  - Incumbent: $400K (discounted) + $200K integration = $600K
  - Us: $500K + $50K integration = $550K + better ROI
- Champion presented to CFO (we armed him with data)

---

## Results (30-Day Follow-up)

| Metric | Before | After | Change |
|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| **Days in Proposal** | 62 | 18 (to close) | Deal accelerated |
| **MEDDIC Score** | 6/10 | 9/10 | +3 pts |
| **Legal Blockers** | 15 questions | 0 | Resolved |
| **CFO Engagement** | None | 2 meetings | Exec buy-in |

**Outcome:**
- Deal closed at $480K (4% discount, not 30%)
- Legal playbook now standard (prevents future stalls)
- Exec-to-exec escalation playbook documented

---

## Key Lessons

1. **Stalled proposal = process blocker, not price** - Champion couldn't navigate legal/CFO
2. **Arm your champion** - Legal FAQ + ROI calc gave him ammo
3. **Exec escalation works** - VP-to-CFO unlocked the deal
4. **Competitive defense = TCO, not price match** - Total cost narrative beat discount

**Playbook update:** If proposal >30 days, trigger executive alignment + legal support offer
