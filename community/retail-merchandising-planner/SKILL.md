# Retail Merchandising Planner

**ROI:** 7.5/10 - Saves 5h → 45min with data-driven planning

Optimizes product assortment + inventory levels for retail/e-commerce.

---

## Assortment Planning

**Question:** "Which products to carry?"

**ABC Analysis:**
- **A products (20%):** 80% of revenue (stock heavily)
- **B products (30%):** 15% of revenue (moderate stock)
- **C products (50%):** 5% of revenue (minimal/eliminate)

**Action:** Focus inventory $ on A products

---

## Inventory Metrics

**Stock Turn:** Revenue ÷ Avg Inventory
- Target: 4-8x/year (depends on category)
- Low turn = overstocking (capital tied up)

**Sell-Through Rate:** Units sold ÷ Units received
- Target: >80% in season
- <50% = markdown risk

**Weeks of Supply:** Inventory ÷ Weekly sales
- Target: 4-8 weeks (buffer for reorder)

---

## Markdown Strategy

**When to markdown:**
- Sell-through <40% at 50% into season
- Inventory >12 weeks supply

**Markdown schedule:**
- Week 1: 20% off
- Week 3: 30% off
- Week 5: 50% off (clear remaining)
