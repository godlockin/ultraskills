# Brand Messaging Framework

**ROI:** 8.5/10 - Saves 6h → 1h with structured messaging hierarchy

Creates consistent brand voice from positioning to taglines.

---

## Messaging Hierarchy

```
1. Positioning Statement (Internal)
   ↓
2. Value Proposition (Customer-facing)
   ↓
3. Tagline (Brand essence)
   ↓
4. Pillar Messages (3-5 key themes)
   ↓
5. Proof Points (Evidence for each pillar)
```

---

## 1. Positioning Statement

**Template:** For [target], who [need], [product] is [category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [competitor], we [differentiation].

**Example (Slack):**
"For teams who need to communicate efficiently, Slack is a collaboration platform that brings all conversations into one place. Unlike email, Slack organizes discussions by channel and integrates with your tools."

---

## 2. Value Proposition

**Template:** We help [customer] [achieve goal] by [unique approach].

**Example:** "We help sales teams close deals faster by showing exactly which leads to prioritize."

---

## 3. Tagline

**Criteria:** 3-5 words, memorable, differentiated

**Examples:**
- Nike: "Just Do It"
- Apple: "Think Different"
- Mailchimp: "Make Things Happen"

---

## 4. Pillar Messages (3-5)

**Example (CRM product):**
1. **Save Time:** Automate manual reporting
2. **Close More:** AI-powered lead scoring
3. **Forecast Better:** Real-time pipeline visibility

---

## 5. Proof Points

**Pillar: Save Time**
- Proof: "Customers save 10h/week on reporting"
- Case study: "[Company X] cut admin time 80%"
- Feature: "Auto-generate reports in 1 click"

---

## Voice & Tone Guidelines

**Voice (consistent):** How brand always sounds
- Example: "Friendly but professional, data-driven"

**Tone (varies by context):**
- Landing page: Confident, aspirational
- Support docs: Clear, helpful
- Error messages: Empathetic, solution-focused
