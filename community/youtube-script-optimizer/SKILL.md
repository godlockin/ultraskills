# YouTube Script Optimizer

**ROI:** 8.0/10 - Saves 2h → 20min with retention-optimized scripts

Applies YouTube algorithm + psychology principles for higher retention.

---

## Script Structure (8-12 min video)

### 0:00-0:15 - Hook (CRITICAL)

**Goal:** Stop the scroll in first 3 seconds

**Formula:**
- Tease the payoff: "By end of this video, you'll [specific outcome]"
- Pattern interrupt: Unexpected statement/visual
- Question hook: "Ever wondered why [intriguing question]?"

**Bad hook:** "Hey guys, today we're talking about..."
**Good hook:** "This one sales trick earned me $100K in 30 days. Here's how."

---

### 0:15-1:00 - Context + Promise

- Why this matters (problem/pain)
- What you'll learn (3 key points)
- Credibility signal (why trust me?)

**Example:**
"Most sales reps lose deals in the demo. I analyzed 500 demos and found the 3 mistakes killing 80% of them. I'll show you each one + the fix."

---

### 1:00-8:00 - Main Content (Value Delivery)

**Structure:** Rule of 3
1. Point 1 (2 min)
   - Concept explanation
   - Example/story
   - Actionable tip
2. Point 2 (2 min)
3. Point 3 (2 min)

**Retention tactics:**
- B-roll every 3-5 seconds (visual variety)
- Pattern interrupt every 60 sec (joke, graphic, music change)
- Open loops: "More on this in a second..."

---

### 8:00-9:00 - Recap + CTA

- **Recap:** "So remember: [3 points summarized]"
- **CTA:** "If this helped, subscribe for weekly [topic] tips"
- **Next video tease:** "Next week: [related topic]"

**CTA placement:** Ask for sub AFTER delivering value, not before

---

## Retention Optimization Checklist

- [ ] Hook in first 3 seconds
- [ ] Promise payoff by 0:30
- [ ] No fluff (cut "um", long intros)
- [ ] B-roll every 3-5 sec
- [ ] Pattern interrupt every 60 sec
- [ ] Clear structure (Rule of 3)
- [ ] Recap at end
- [ ] CTA after value delivery

---

## Example Optimization

**Before (2:30 avg watch time, 30% retention):**
"Hey everyone, welcome back to my channel. Today I want to talk about something really important - sales objections. So first, let me tell you a story..."

**After (6:00 avg watch time, 65% retention):**
"This one sentence closes 80% of objections. [PAUSE] Here's the 3-step framework:
1. [Point]
2. [Point]
3. [Point]

Let me show you exactly how..."

**Changes:**
- Hook: Specific claim (80%), pattern interrupt (pause)
- Structure: Promised 3 steps upfront
- Cut: Removed "welcome", "let me tell you"

---

## YouTube Algorithm Signals

**High retention = more reach**

- Watch time (total minutes)
- Avg view duration (% of video)
- Click-through rate (thumbnail + title)
- Comments/likes (engagement)

**Target:** >50% avg view duration (8-12 min video)
