# Pitch Deck Critic

**ROI:** 9.0/10 - Transforms 10h of iteration to 1h with VC-tested feedback

Expert pitch deck review using VC evaluation frameworks. Catches fatal flaws before investor meetings.

---

## When to Use

- **Pre-fundraise:** Before sending deck to investors
- **Post-meeting:** Iterate based on VC feedback
- **Founder coaching:** Train on what investors look for
- **Competitive review:** Benchmark against funded decks

---

## Standard Deck Structure (Sequoia Model)

**10-15 slides, 15-20 min presentation:**

1. **Problem** (1 slide) - Pain point, market friction
2. **Solution** (1 slide) - Your product/service
3. **Why Now** (1 slide) - Market timing, tailwinds
4. **Market Size** (1 slide) - TAM/SAM/SOM
5. **Product** (2-3 slides) - Demo, differentiation
6. **Traction** (1-2 slides) - Metrics, logos, growth
7. **Business Model** (1 slide) - Unit economics, pricing
8. **Team** (1 slide) - Founders, key hires, advisors
9. **Competition** (1 slide) - Landscape, positioning
10. **Financials** (1 slide) - 3-year projections
11. **Ask** (1 slide) - Raise amount, use of funds

## Review Criteria (VC Lens)

### 1. Problem Clarity (0-10)

**Score 9-10:** Visceral pain, quantified cost, urgent need
**Score 5-6:** Generic problem, no urgency
**Score 0-2:** Solution in search of problem

**Red flags:**
- "Nice to have" not "must have"
- No market validation
- Founder assumption, not customer research

---

### 2. Solution Differentiation (0-10)

**Score 9-10:** 10x better, unfair advantage, hard to replicate
**Score 5-6:** Incremental improvement, competitive
**Score 0-2:** "Me too" product

**Test:** "Why can't [Big Tech Co] build this in 6 months?"

---

### 3. Market Opportunity (0-10)

**Score 9-10:** $1B+ TAM, growing >20% YoY, fragmented
**Score 5-6:** $100M TAM, mature market
**Score 0-2:** Niche, declining

**Formula check:**
- TAM (Total Addressable Market): Everyone who could buy
- SAM (Serviceable Available Market): Realistic segment
- SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market): Year 5 capture

**Red flag:** TAM >$100B (too broad), SOM >30% (unrealistic)

---

### 4. Traction Proof (0-10)

**Score 9-10:** Revenue, retention, NPS >50, logo momentum
**Score 5-6:** Users, no revenue, unclear engagement
**Score 0-2:** Concept, no customers

**Seed stage:** 10K+ users OR $10K+ MRR OR 3 design partners
**Series A:** $1M+ ARR, 100%+ YoY growth, <10% churn

---

### 5. Team Credibility (0-10)

**Score 9-10:** Domain expert, repeat founder, or A-player team
**Score 5-6:** Smart but unproven
**Score 0-2:** Missing key roles, no relevant experience

**Investor question:** "Why is this team uniquely positioned to win?"

---

### 6. Business Model Clarity (0-10)

**Score 9-10:** Unit economics proven, CAC payback <12mo, LTV:CAC >3x
**Score 5-6:** Business model defined, economics TBD
**Score 0-2:** "We'll figure it out later"

**Formula:**
- LTV (Lifetime Value) = ARPA ÷ Churn Rate
- CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) = Sales + Marketing spend ÷ New customers
- **Target:** LTV ÷ CAC >3x, CAC payback <12 months

---

### 7. Ask + Use of Funds (0-10)

**Score 9-10:** Specific raise amount, milestone-based use, 18-24mo runway
**Score 5-6:** Vague amount, generic use ("hiring, marketing")
**Score 0-2:** No ask, or "as much as we can get"

**Best practice:**
- Raise amount: 18-24 months runway
- Use of funds: 3-5 buckets with $ allocation
- Milestones: What you'll achieve with this raise

## Common Fatal Flaws

### Flaw 1: "Solution in Search of Problem"

**Symptom:** 5 slides on product, 0.5 slides on problem

**Fix:**
- Start with customer pain (quote real user)
- Quantify cost of problem ($X lost annually)
- Show failed alternatives (what they tried before you)

---

### Flaw 2: "Hockey Stick Projections"

**Symptom:** Year 1: $100K → Year 3: $50M (500x growth)

**Fix:**
- Bottom-up forecast (unit economics × realistic adoption)
- Comparable benchmarks (similar companies at this stage)
- Acknowledge risks (what could go wrong)

---

### Flaw 3: "Visionary Founder, No Traction"

**Symptom:** 10 years experience, 0 customers

**Fix:**
- Even 5 beta users > 0
- Show customer discovery process (100 interviews)
- Pivot story (what you learned, how you adapted)

---

### Flaw 4: "Competitive Landscape = Magic Quadrant"

**Symptom:** 2x2 chart with "us" in top-right corner alone

**Fix:**
- Honest competitive assessment
- Why customers choose you (not feature comparison)
- Unfair advantage (network effects, data moat, team)
