# OKR Alignment Checker

**ROI:** 7.5/10 - Saves 2h → 10min validating goal cascades

Ensures company/team/individual OKRs align without conflicts.

---

## OKR Structure

**Objective:** Qualitative goal (inspiring, ambitious)
**Key Results:** Quantitative metrics (measurable, time-bound)

**Example:**
- **O:** Become market leader in [segment]
- **KR1:** Increase market share from 15% → 25%
- **KR2:** Achieve 90+ NPS (top quartile)
- **KR3:** $10M ARR (+100% YoY)

---

## Alignment Check

### Level 1: Company OKRs

**O:** Achieve product-market fit in enterprise segment
- **KR1:** $5M ARR from enterprise customers
- **KR2:** 3 case studies from Fortune 500
- **KR3:** <5% enterprise churn

### Level 2: Sales Team OKRs

**O:** Build repeatable enterprise sales motion
- **KR1:** Close 10 enterprise deals ($500K+ each)
- **KR2:** Reduce sales cycle to <90 days
- **KR3:** Achieve 30% win rate (enterprise)

✅ **Alignment check:** Sales KR1 (10 deals × $500K) = $5M → supports Company KR1

### Level 3: Individual AE OKRs

**O:** Master enterprise sales process
- **KR1:** Close 2 enterprise deals personally
- **KR2:** Achieve $1M personal quota
- **KR3:** Maintain >80 MEDDIC score on all opps

✅ **Alignment check:** 5 AEs × 2 deals = 10 total → supports Sales KR1

## Misalignment Detection

### Anti-Pattern 1: Conflicting Priorities

**Company OKR:** Increase revenue +50%
**Product OKR:** Rebuild platform (6-month project)

❌ **Conflict:** Platform rebuild delays revenue features

**Fix:** Either:
- Prioritize revenue (delay rebuild)
- Adjust revenue goal (acknowledge rebuild impact)

---

### Anti-Pattern 2: Metric Gaming

**Support KR:** Reduce avg ticket resolution time to <4 hours

❌ **Gaming risk:** Close tickets prematurely without solving issue

**Fix:** Add quality metric
- **KR1:** <4 hour resolution
- **KR2:** >90% CSAT (prevents gaming)

---

### Anti-Pattern 3: Sandbagging

**Sales KR:** Achieve $10M ARR

❌ **Sandbagging:** Current run-rate already $9M (only +11% stretch)

**Fix:** True stretch goal = 2-3x current capability
- Better KR: $15M ARR (+67%)

---

## Validation Checklist

- [ ] Company OKRs → Team OKRs aligned
- [ ] Team OKRs → Individual OKRs aligned
- [ ] No conflicting priorities
- [ ] Metrics can't be gamed
- [ ] Stretch goals (not sandbagging)
- [ ] 3-5 KRs per Objective (not 10+)
- [ ] Measurable outcomes (not activities)
