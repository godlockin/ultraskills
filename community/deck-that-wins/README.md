# deck-that-wins

> Build competition presentation decks that actually win — not just look good.

## Why This Exists

Lost a competition with strong content. Analyzed every winner. Found 6 patterns we missed. Codified them so it never happens again.

## What's Inside

- **6 Iron Rules** — carrier wins battle, content wins war; team personality = 24h memorability; market identity > project status; 5-home balance; findings not self-talk; sensory debt compounds
- **8 Pass Criteria** — binary gates: HTML carrier, 5-country balance, zero sensory debt, team personality, ≤1 self-talk page, audio baseline, multi-speaker, bilingual
- **8 Bonus Items** — market identity quotable, cultural pun name, video format, counter-intuitive hook, QR link, hover interaction, AI voice, closing quotable
- **Day-0 Checklist** — 7 items to nail before you start analyzing

## When to Use

- Competition submission (hackathon, case comp, pitch)
- High-stakes presentation where you're being compared to others
- Auditing an existing deck before submission
- Training a team on what separates winners from runners-up

## Quick Start

Read the 6 iron rules. Score your deck 1–10 on each. Anything below 7 is where you focus.
