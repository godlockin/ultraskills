# Onboarding Checklist Generator

**ROI:** 7.5/10 - Saves 2h → 15min creating role-specific onboarding

Generates day-by-day onboarding checklists for new hires.

---

## Onboarding Timeline

### Pre-Day 1 (Week before start)

**HR tasks:**
- [ ] Send welcome email (login credentials, first-day logistics)
- [ ] Provision laptop + accounts (Slack, email, tools)
- [ ] Assign onboarding buddy
- [ ] Schedule Week 1 meetings (manager, team, HR)

**Manager tasks:**
- [ ] Prepare workspace (desk, welcome gift)
- [ ] Define 30-60-90 day goals
- [ ] Create first project assignment

---

### Day 1: Company Orientation

**Morning:**
- [ ] Office tour + introductions (9am)
- [ ] IT setup session (10am)
- [ ] HR benefits overview (11am)
- [ ] Team lunch (12pm)

**Afternoon:**
- [ ] Company vision presentation (CEO, 1pm)
- [ ] Product demo (2pm)
- [ ] Manager 1:1 - role expectations (3pm)
- [ ] End-of-day check-in (4:30pm)

---

### Week 1: Foundational Learning

**Goals:**
- Understand company mission, products, culture
- Meet key stakeholders (cross-functional)
- Complete compliance training
- Shadow team members

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Complete HRIS profile
- [ ] Finish compliance training (security, harassment, etc.)
- [ ] Read company handbook
- [ ] Meet 10 people outside immediate team

---

### Week 2-4: Role Immersion

**Goals:**
- Start contributing (small projects)
- Learn tools + systems
- Receive coaching from buddy/manager

**Deliverables (Week 4):**
- [ ] Complete first project
- [ ] Present learnings to team
- [ ] Manager feedback session

---

### Day 30/60/90: Milestone Check-ins

**30-day review:**
- Comfort with role? (1-10 scale)
- Blockers/questions?
- Adjust goals if needed

**60-day review:**
- Performance vs 60-day goals
- Peer feedback (360 lite)
- Career development discussion

**90-day review:**
- Full performance evaluation
- Long-term goals alignment
- Probation completion (if applicable)

---

## Role-Specific Additions

**Sales rep:**
- [ ] Shadow 5 customer calls
- [ ] Complete sales enablement training
- [ ] First deal (assisted close)

**Engineer:**
- [ ] Codebase walkthrough
- [ ] Deploy to production (shadowed)
- [ ] Fix first bug

**Customer Success:**
- [ ] QBR shadowing (3 accounts)
- [ ] Customer playbook training
- [ ] First customer call (own)
